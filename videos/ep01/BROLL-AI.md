## If a clip comes out wrong
- **Phone held to the ear (clip 1):** add "both hands on the phone, looking down at the screen".
- **Garbled text or signs:** add "plain walls, no writing anywhere". Avoid words like brochure, board or sign.
- **A face drifts:** regenerate from the family picture (Image to video), not from text.
- **Six fingers or melting hands:** pick a take where the hands are still, or ask for "hands relaxed, out of focus".
