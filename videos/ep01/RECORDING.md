## Setup: full body, hands visible (new for this series)
You stand, and the whole of you is in the frame with room to move your hands.

- **Framing:** landscape 16:9. Stand about 2.5–3 m from the camera, with the camera at chest height. Leave space above
  your head (about a hand's width) and below your feet, and keep both arms in frame when stretched out to the side.
- **Stand in the left third of the frame** (your body centred around x ≈ 30%). The right side stays free for the
  animations, so nothing has to cover you.
- **Camera:** a phone on a tripod at **4K 30 fps** is best: 4K lets the edit reframe gently without losing your
  hands. The laptop webcam at 1080p works too, but then the camera stays fixed for the whole video.
- **Mic: needed now.** At 3 m the camera mic sounds far away and echoey. Use a clip-on (lavalier) mic or a phone
  recording in your pocket. If it is a separate file, clap once on camera at the start so the two line up.

- Plain background, bright even light on the front of you (your purple LED cove lights behind are fine).
- No background removal. Same framing for the whole session, a second of silence at each end.
- Fluffed a line? Say **"cut this line"**, pause ~2 s, restart the whole sentence. The last take wins.
- If a number comes out wrong, redo the whole sentence.
- No notes to yourself on mic; clap if you need a marker.

## Edit rules for the full (long) video only: full body, never cropped
These apply to the YouTube long video only and replace the usual talking-head rules wherever they clash. The Short
follows its own rule (below).
- **You stay whole in every frame:** head, hands and feet are never cropped, by a zoom, a card or the 9:16 reframe.
- **No punch-ins or creeps on you.** Camera moves only on 4K footage, at most 1.05, and only if your outstretched
  hands still fit (checked on a snapshot of the widest gesture in that stretch). On 1080p footage the camera stays at 100%.
- **Animations live in the free right side** (x > ~45%), never over your body. Instead of full-frame cutaways,
  the big moments (budget bars, goal timeline, the ₹12,00,000 / ₹39,00,000 counters) use a split layout: you on the
  left, the animation on the right, so you stay on screen the whole video.
- **Lower lines go under the animation side,** not across your legs.
- **Retakes still get removed** (your "cut this line" spots and long pauses). The rest is kept as it is.
