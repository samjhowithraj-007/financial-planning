## Energy checklist (before you press record)
- **Stand up.** Feet apart, camera at eye level. Sitting pulls the voice down.
- **20% louder and a touch faster** than feels natural; it sounds normal on playback.
- **Smile in the hook and the intro**, and let the face react: worried for Amit, happy for Neha.
- **One gesture per punch word**: ₹1 lakh, 31%, ₹58,000, kaafi hai?, ₹12 lakh, ₹39 lakh, sabse pehle safety.
- **Scene by scene**: record each of the 10 scenes 2–3 times in a row, then move on (energy drops in a 10-minute take).
  Fluffed a line? Say "cut this line", pause, restart the sentence. The last good take wins.
- Light in front of you at eye level (a lamp or ring light), black background as before (CapCut Remove BG), 1080p 30 fps.
