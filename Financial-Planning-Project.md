# SamjhoWithRaj — Financial Planning

## Project brief

Series: **Middle-Class Family Ka Money Plan**

Tagline: **Budget se investment tak, ghar se retirement tak—paise ki har baat, aasaan bhasha mein.**

Audience: Middle-class Indian families living in India. This is audience education, not Raj's personal financial plan.

Language: Natural Hindi/Hinglish speech and romanised Hinglish graphics. English website notes.

Format: 50 episodes, approximately 6–8 minutes each, with one optional Short per episode. Proposed pace: two episodes weekly over 25 weeks; no publication dates or automations have been scheduled.

Recurring fictional household: Two adults, two children, ₹1,00,000 monthly household take-home income. Use occasional ₹30,000 and ₹60,000 scenarios. These are examples, not verified national averages. Parents' ages, children’s ages, city and starting assets must be explicitly chosen for each calculation and then kept consistent across related episodes.

## Production rules

- Explain as if talking to a friend over chai. Start with a real family problem, use one numerical example and finish with one useful action.
- Use the imported youtube-edit skill for video planning and editing; preserve channel colours, readable text, Hinglish delivery and speech-synchronised visuals.
- Keep this finance series separate from Python Chai and AI Chai Bot episode numbering.
- For each script, verify current tax, interest-rate, investment, loan and regulatory claims from relevant primary sources. Record source links and the date checked.
- Label every scenario and projection as illustrative. State horizon, inflation, returns, fees/taxes treatment and contribution timing. Recheck arithmetic.
- Avoid guaranteed-return claims and fabricated proof, balances or earnings. Match risk to the goal and deadline.
- Use only supplied or appropriately licensed footage and music. Verify social links before publication.
- Creating scripts or assets does not publish them. Publishing needs an explicit publishing request and access to the destination.

## Repeatable episode template

1. **Brief:** Episode number, audience problem, one learning outcome and continuity notes.
2. **Research:** Claims, primary sources, dates checked and unresolved questions.
3. **Calculation sheet:** Inputs, formulas, assumptions and independently checked outputs.
4. **Hook:** A relatable problem and an honest promise in the first 15 seconds.
5. **Talking points:** Three simple ideas with the recurring family example.
6. **Storyboard:** Timestamp, spoken point, on-screen text, graphic and source/asset.
7. **Recording notes:** Natural Hinglish prompts and clean retake markers.
8. **Edit:** Timing, readable visuals, audio balance and checked calculations on screen.
9. **Packaging:** Title, thumbnail text, YouTube description, Short, Instagram and Facebook captions.
10. **Review:** Facts, arithmetic, clarity, audio/video quality and publishing destination.
